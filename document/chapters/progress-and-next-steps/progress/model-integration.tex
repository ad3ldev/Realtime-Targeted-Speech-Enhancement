\subsection{Model Integration}

As mentioned on the previous section, we used PyTorch as our deep learning framework. We integrated 2 model architectures into our project structure: \textbf{DEMUCS} (Denoiser) \cite{defossez2020} and \textbf{CleanUNet} \cite{kong2022}.

For DEMUCS, we used the implementation provided by Meta, the authors of the paper. The implementation is available on GitHub: \texttt{\url{https://github.com/facebookresearch/denoiser}}. We also used the official implementation of CleanUNet provided by Nvidia, the authors of the paper. The implementation is available on GitHub: \texttt{\url{https://github.com/NVIDIA/CleanUNet}}.
