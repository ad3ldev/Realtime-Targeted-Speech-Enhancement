\chapter{Introduction}
\label{chap:intro}

\begingroup
\let\clearpage\relax

Speech enhancement is a critical domain within signal processing, dedicated to refining speech signals captured under adverse conditions marked by noise or degradation. The core objective is the enhancement of quality and intelligibility in these speech signals. The Real-time Targeted Speech Enhancement project aims to address the challenge of enhancing speech signals in real-time with a reference of clear speech provided by the user. 

The overarching aim of speech enhancement is to elevate the clarity and understandability of speech, particularly in challenging environments. In many applications, a fundamental requirement for a speech enhancement system is its ability to operate in real-time with minimal latency, preferably on cost-effective hardware integrated into communication devices. The quality of speech in virtual interactions significantly impacts user experience and effective communication.

This document presents an in-depth exploration of the problem definition, background information, related work, progress made so far, and the next steps in the project. The subsequent chapters will provide a comprehensive overview of the problem domain, the current state of the art in speech enhancement, the experimental setup, progress made in the project, and the planned next steps.

\hyperref[chap:problem-def]{Chapter 2} will focus on the problem definition, outlining the core objectives of the project and the challenges associated with speech enhancement in the context of online communication. The chapter will also highlight the importance of leveraging deep learning methods specifically tailored for real-time speech enhancement.

\hyperref[chap:background]{Chapter 3} will delve into the fundamental concepts of audio representation, including waveforms, time-frequency representations, and output representations. The chapter will also explore the application of deep learning methodologies, such as Convolutional Neural Networks and Recurrent Neural Networks, in speech enhancement and audio processing.

\hyperref[chap:datasets]{Chapter 4} will discuss the datasets used in the project, including the Valentini dataset, the DNS dataset and the LibriSpeech dataset. The chapter will also provide an overview of the data augmentation techniques used to increase the size of the training data.

\hyperref[chap:metrics]{Chapter 5} will present the evaluation metrics used to assess the performance of the speech enhancement models including both objective and subjective metrics. It will introduce the Perceptual Evaluation of Speech Quality (PESQ) and the Short-Time Objective Intelligibility (STOI) metrics and other metrics used in the evaluation of speech enhancement models.

\hyperref[chap:related-work]{Chapter 6} will provide an overview of the related work in speech enhancement, highlighting the current state of the art and the gaps in the existing literature. The chapter will also discuss the potential impact of the Real-time Targeted Speech Enhancement project on the field of speech enhancement.

\hyperref[chap:progress-and-next-steps]{Chapter 7} will focus on the progress made so far in the project, including the development of deep learning models for speech enhancement and the evaluation of their performance. The chapter will also discuss the challenges encountered during the project and the strategies used to overcome them. This chapter will also outline the planned next steps in the project, including the refinement of the existing models, the exploration of new deep learning architectures, the evaluation of the models on new datasets, and the development of the application.

By the end of this document, readers will gain valuable insights into the challenges, methodologies, and advancements in real-time targeted speech enhancement.
