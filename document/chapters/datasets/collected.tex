\section{Collected Dataset}
\subsection{Motivation}
The DNS dataset was massively collected, and its training samples were not a clear representation of the problem that we tried to solve. DNS dataset follows the quantity over quality principle
which have been used frequently in the deep learning community for a fast and cheap data collection process.
Due to limited computing resources, we started by training our models on only 100 hours from 500 hours of the DNS dataset. The results of these experiments
were not satisfying since the underlying dataset had neither quality nor quantity.
