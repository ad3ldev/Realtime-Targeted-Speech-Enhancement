\section{Model Selection}

To assess the performance of our models we compared the results of our models against the top 7 models submitted to the DNS2023 challenge and the baseline model provided by the challenge organizers.
Table \ref{tab:models-dns2023-testset} shows the results of the models on the DNS2023 blind test set and their ranking among other models based on OVRL MOS.
Despite the limited data that our models were trained on compared to the other models, our models achieved competitive results.
The Personalized Fast FullSubNet model ranked in the $6^{th}$ place and PDenoiser got the $2^{nd}$ place. This result shows that our models and cleaner dataset are capable to compete with models trained on much larger datasets.

For further evaluation, we compared the results of our models on our collected test set at an SNR of 6. The results are shown in table \ref{tab:models-our-testset}.
The Personalized Denoiser model outperformerd the Personalized Fast FullSubNet model in all the metrics.

Based on these results we decided to select the Personalized Denoiser model as our final model for the project. The model will be integrated into the application for a lightweight, personalized and real-time noise suppression solution.
