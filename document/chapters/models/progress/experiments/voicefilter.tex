\subsection{VoiceFilter}
The experiments for VoiceFilter \cite{wang2019voicefilter} started before the completion of the project structure. Therefore, VoiceFilter experiments were conducted outside the project structure. We used an unofficial implementation of VoiceFilter available on GitHub: \texttt{\url{https://github.com/maum-ai/voicefilter}}. Although the implementation is unofficial and not provided by Google, the authors of the paper, it was acknowledged by the authors in the latest version of the paper.

Unfortunately, we couldn't reproduce the results of the paper. Our experiments lasted 108 hours. We operated on the same subset of LibriSpeech dataset \cite{librispeech} as the paper. We trained the model using a single P100 GPU.
