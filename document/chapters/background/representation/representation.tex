\section{Audio Representation \cite{manilow2020}}
Let's begin by examining the input and output representations used in speech enhancement systems. In its rawest form, we generally work with audio stored as a waveform. While some speech enhancement techniques operate directly on this waveform, many require preprocessing steps before the actual noise reduction process. This section will explore the different types of input and output representations commonly employed in speech enhancement approaches.

We'll first delve into waveforms, arguably the most fundamental representation of audio. Then, we'll dive into some common audio representations specifically utilized for speech enhancement tasks.
