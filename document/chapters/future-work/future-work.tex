\chapter{Future Work}
\label{chap:future-work}
This chapter discusses the potential future work and improvements that can be made to the project.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Increasing Data Size:}
          \begin{itemize}
              \item Enhance the dataset quality by incorporating additional speakers and a wider range of languages.
          \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Data Synthesis:}
          \begin{itemize}
              \item Synthesize data with varying noise levels and diverse noise types to increase the model's robustness.
          \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Handling Noisy Reference Audio:}
          \begin{itemize}
              \item Develop strategies to make the model resilient to noisy reference audio inputs.
          \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Different Sampling Rates Support:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item The suggested models are currently designed to work with audio sampled at 16kHz. Extend support to include other sampling rates such as 44.1 kHz and 48 kHz.
        \end{itemize}
